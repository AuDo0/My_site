== accueil ==
Q: How do I ask my email and documents with AI?
A: Connect your mailboxes and file spaces, choose which folders to read, then ask your question in plain language. {name} searches your email and documents, writes the answer and cites every passage it used. Clicking a citation opens the document on the right page.

Q: What is {name}, in one sentence?
A: An assistant connected to your email and documents: it answers your questions by citing its sources, keeps the memory of every folder and turns it into your tasks and deadlines. Your documents remain the single source of truth.

Q: How is it different from searching my mailbox?
A: A search gives you a list of emails to reread. {name} reads those emails and your documents for you, writes the answer and cites every passage it used. It also keeps each folder's memory: timeline, people, key facts, versions.

Q: What if the information doesn't exist?
A: {name} says so. It never fills an answer with made-up information: no source, no answer.

Q: Does {name} act without asking me?
A: No. It suggests (a deadline, a task, a folder update) and you approve. Routines, which will be able to act on their own with a log of everything done, are coming soon.

Q: Do I need to install anything?
A: No. {name} is a web app: a browser is enough, on computer, tablet or phone. Installing it on your phone's home screen is coming soon.

Q: Is the demo data real?
A: No. Every name, folder, email and document in the demo is made up.

== connecteurs ==
Q: Which email services and file spaces can I connect?
A: Today: Outlook, Gmail, Google Drive and Dropbox. Calendar and audio recordings are in the works.

Q: Does {name} read my whole mailbox?
A: No. You choose which mailboxes, folders and labels are read. Everything else is neither read nor copied.

Q: Can I remove a connector?
A: Yes, at any time. Access is cut off and that source's index is erased.

Q: Can {name} send emails or change my files?
A: Access is read-only. {name} prepares drafts or suggestions (a deadline, a task, an email to link to its folder), which you approve.

Q: My tool isn't listed. What can I do?
A: Request it with the form on this page. We build connectors in order of demand, and custom connectors are possible for business tools.

== routines ==
Q: Are routines available?
A: Not yet: they are coming soon. This page shows how they will work, with made-up data.

Q: How do I file my email automatically?
A: With a routine, coming soon. You will write it once ("when an invoice arrives from this supplier, file it in Suppliers / name / year / month"), then {name} will apply it to every new email. You will choose whether it asks for your approval each time. Meanwhile, {name} already suggests linking each email to its folder.

Q: What is a routine?
A: A rule you write once: when this happens, if this condition is met, then do that. For example: "when I receive an offer from this supplier, file it in Offers / supplier / year / month".

Q: Does a routine act without my approval?
A: You will choose, routine by routine: either {name} asks for your approval each time, or it acts on its own and records everything in the log.

Q: Can I undo what a routine did?
A: Yes. The log will show each action, its date and its reason, and let you roll it back.

Q: Who creates routines?
A: You, with the editor. {name} will also be able to suggest some when it notices you repeat the same action; nothing will switch on without your consent.

Q: Can I schedule a routine, for example every evening?
A: Later: scheduled routines (such as a summary of unread email every evening) will follow the first routines.

== domaines ==
Q: Is {name} suited to lawyers and researchers?
A: Yes. For lawyers, {name} adds published statutes and case law, and links new rulings to your cases. For researchers, it adds open paper databases and your bibliography. Either way, every answer cites its sources.

Q: What is a field-specific version?
A: The same assistant, plus your profession's references: statutes and case law for legal work, open paper databases for research, your own standards and frameworks for business.

Q: What does "connected live" mean?
A: For legal and research, {name} queries up-to-date public databases and analyses new publications as they come out.

Q: What is passive monitoring?
A: {name} checks every new publication against your folders. When one concerns a folder (a new ruling, a new paper), it tells you, with the source and the reason. You decide whether to add it to the folder or dismiss it. Available for legal work; for research, passive monitoring is coming soon.

Q: What about standards for businesses?
A: You add your standards and frameworks as documents. {name} cites them in its answers like any other source.

Q: My field isn't listed.
A: Each version is built to measure: vocabulary, rules, document templates, connectors, frameworks. Write to us from "Talk to the team".
